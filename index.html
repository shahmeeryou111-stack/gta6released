GTA 6 Release Date — When Is It Coming Out?

Let's cut through the noise. Grand Theft Auto VI is officially releasing on November 19, 2026, exclusively on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch. Rockstar Games confirmed the date in their official announcement, and they have re-affirmed it at every subsequent earnings call held by parent company Take-Two Interactive. There is no ambiguity about the release date.

Rockstar's exact wording matters, because Rockstar chooses its wording carefully. In their announcement they wrote:

"We are proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI will release on November 19, 2026."— Sam Houser, President of Rockstar Games

That single sentence launched a thousand Reddit threads. Rockstar has now confirmed the specific release date of November 19, 2026, giving fans a concrete target to look forward to.

Why November 19, 2026?

November 19, 2026 falls on a Thursday, which is a strategic choice for Rockstar. This date positions the launch just before the Thanksgiving holiday weekend in the United States, maximizing initial sales momentum. It also gives Rockstar ample time to polish the game while still capturing the crucial holiday shopping season.

Rockstar historically favors Tuesdays for launches (retail cycle alignment), but a Thursday launch for GTA 6 suggests confidence in the game's quality and the desire to build weekend buzz through social media and streaming platforms.

Rockstar's Delay History — Should We Worry?

Here's the uncomfortable truth: Rockstar has delayed every single one of their last four flagship games. GTA V slipped from Spring 2013 to September 2013. Red Dead Redemption 2 slipped twice, from Fall 2017 to Spring 2018 to October 2018. GTA V's PC port slipped from January 2015 to April 2015. The pattern is real.

That said, there are strong reasons to believe GTA 6 will not slip out of 2026:

  • Take-Two has staked its fiscal year on it. The company's public financial guidance for FY2027 (ending March 2027) is built around a November 2026 launch. A slip would trigger a stock event.
  • Development has been ongoing since 2014. By launch, GTA 6 will have been in active development for over a decade — longer than any other AAA title ever.
  • Rockstar's messaging is unusually confident. Sam Houser rarely publicly commits to windows this narrow.

Every Credible Leak About The Date

Not every "leak" is worth reading. We only track sources with a verified track record. Here are the credible ones:

  • Jason Schreier (Bloomberg) — reported that internal targets pointed at "November 2026." Schreier is the single most reliable source on Rockstar.
  • Take-Two 10-Q filings — repeated references to "our largest planned title" launching in fiscal 2027 (April 2026 – March 2027).
  • Tom Henderson (Insider Gaming) — corroborated the November 2026 date, adding that a launch trailer was expected roughly six months before release.

November 19, 2026 By Region

Grand Theft Auto launches globally. Expect essentially simultaneous availability across every region, subject to time zones:

  • United States: Midnight local time launch (12:00 AM local on release day)
  • United Kingdom & EU: Same-day launch, midnight local
  • Australia & Japan: Same-day launch — often ahead of North America because of the international date line

Digital pre-loads typically unlock 48 hours before launch, so plan your download accordingly — this game is going to be enormous.

Why Rockstar Chose November 19, 2026

Three overlapping reasons: financial, competitive, and cultural. Financially, holiday season sales dwarf every other quarter. Competitively, no other publisher will dare drop a AAA title in GTA 6's release window — the calendar clears around Rockstar. Culturally, fall is when the industry pays attention: The Game Awards, year-end lists, and every "Game of the Year" conversation happen in Q4.

What Platforms Is GTA 6 Coming To?

Let's answer the biggest platform questions in order of how often they land in our inbox: PC, PS4, and Nintendo Switch 2. But first, the confirmed launch platforms.

Confirmed: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

Grand Theft Auto VI will launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. This includes the PS5 Pro (with likely enhanced visuals/frame rate) and the Xbox Series S (which will run a scaled-down version, as it does with every current-gen title). Both digital and physical editions are expected.

PC Release — The Big Question

Rockstar has not confirmed a PC version at launch, and it almost certainly will not launch simultaneously. Here's the history that predicts this:

  • GTA V — Console: September 2013. PC: April 2015. 19 months late.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 — Console: October 2018. PC: November 2019. 13 months late.
  • L.A. Noire — Console: May 2011. PC: November 2011. 6 months late.

The pattern is clear: expect the PC port between 6 and 19 months after console launch. Our best estimate is a PC release between Spring 2027 and Winter 2027.

Why Rockstar Delays PC Releases

Rockstar's strategy of console-first, PC-later isn't accidental. It maximizes revenue by getting console owners to buy first (they can't wait for a PC version), then re-selling to PC owners years later with enhancements. It also gives them time to optimize for the vast diversity of PC hardware, which is genuinely hard.

PS4 and Xbox One — Officially Not Coming

Rockstar has confirmed GTA 6 is a current-generation only title. There will be no PS4 or Xbox One version. Ever. This is the right call — the game's world density, physics, and streaming requirements are simply beyond what those consoles can handle.

If you're still on last-gen, this is your official reason to upgrade. PS5 prices routinely dip during holiday sales, and both the standard PS5 and Xbox Series S/X are widely available with no scalper markups anymore.

Nintendo Switch 2 — Possible?

Nintendo's Switch 2 (launched 2026) is meaningfully more powerful than the original Switch, roughly in the ballpark of an Xbox Series S when docked. A cloud-streamed version is theoretically possible, and a heavily-reduced native port is not out of the question 1-2 years post-launch. That said, Rockstar has never shown much interest in Nintendo platforms — the original Switch received exactly zero Rockstar ports.

GTA 6 Trailers — Every Video Breakdown

The trailer situation is simple: one has dropped, more are coming. But that first trailer contained more information than most people realized, because Rockstar hides an absurd amount of story detail in every second of frame.

GTA VI — Trailer 1 · December 2023

Trailer 1 (December 5, 2023) — Full Breakdown

Rockstar's first trailer was ninety-one seconds long. Every one of those seconds is worth studying. Here's what we saw and what most people missed on first watch.

The location is unambiguously Vice City. Palm trees, art deco buildings, neon signs, alligators, jet skis, thonged beachgoers, strip malls, trailer parks — this is Florida in every frame. Rockstar named the state "Leonida," an anagram-adjacent riff on "Florida," and Vice City itself has been visibly expanded to include not just the strip but also outlying keys, an Everglades-style wetland, and rural interior towns.

Lucia is the first female protagonist in mainline GTA history. The trailer opens on her being processed through a prison, then shows her committing crimes with a male partner across the state. This is enormous — Grand Theft Auto has never centered a woman before, and the framing suggests Rockstar is treating it seriously rather than as a gimmick.

The car chases, the beaches, the pursuits. Every driving shot in the trailer showed dramatically upgraded vehicle physics: proper weight transfer, realistic tire deformation, and lighting that reacted to wet asphalt in ways GTA V could not.

Hidden Details Most People Missed

  • Social media satire is baked in. Multiple scenes show characters recording vertical video for what is clearly an in-game TikTok analogue. Expect this to be a major theme.
  • NPC density is real. Beach shots feature dozens of unique NPCs in different poses, clothing, and body types — a leap over GTA V's crowd rendering.
  • Interiors are seamless. One shot appears to move from an exterior street into a strip club interior with no visible transition. If this holds up in gameplay, it's a technical first for the franchise.
  • Animal AI is dramatically improved. The gator waddling across a road in the second half of the trailer moves with weight and reactive AI that goes beyond GTA V.
  • License plates read "Leonida." This was Rockstar quietly confirming the state name before saying it out loud.

Records Trailer 1 Broke

  • 93 million views in 24 hours on YouTube — a record for a non-music video.
  • #1 trending globally on every major platform within an hour of release.
  • Rockstar's YouTube channel gained more subscribers in 48 hours than in the previous four years combined.

Trailer 2 — Status and Expectations

Trailer 2 has not dropped as of publication. Based on Rockstar's release cadence for previous games, expect it to arrive roughly 3 to 6 months before launch — so a window of May through August 2026. Historically Trailer 2 shows:

  • The protagonists in more detail, including voice lines and dialogue
  • Actual gameplay clips (Trailer 1 was almost entirely in-engine cinematics)
  • The Rockstar Games mission statement text card that traditionally precedes a launch date reveal

Confirmed Screenshots and Media

Alongside the trailer, Rockstar released a handful of high-resolution screenshots showing Vice Beach at golden hour, a Grove Street-style suburban block, and a nightlife exterior with impressive volumetric lighting. Additional media has been drip-fed at Take-Two investor events. Expect a large media dump alongside Trailer 2.

💡 Key Takeaway

Trailer 1 already confirms Vice City, Leonida, the dual protagonists, and a massive visual leap. Trailer 2 is expected between May and August 2026 and will likely include the exact release date, gameplay footage, and pre-order info.

GTA 6 Map — Leonida, Vice City & Beyond

The map is going to be enormous. That's not hype — that's what leaks, Trailer 1 shots, and the technical footprint of the game all point to. Here's every credible datapoint we have on the size and layout of the world.

The Setting: Leonida (Fictional Florida)

Leonida is Rockstar's fictionalized version of Florida, and Vice City is its Miami analog. Unlike GTA V, which was essentially one metro area (Los Santos) plus its rural surroundings (Blaine County), Leonida appears to be an entire state-sized playground with multiple distinct regions.

Map Size Analysis and Comparisons

The most credible sizing estimates, based on the 2022 leak, subsequent Rockstar hires posting on LinkedIn, and comparable analysis of Trailer 1 shots, place the GTA 6 map at approximately two to three times the size of GTA V's Los Santos and Blaine County combined. For reference:

Confirmed Locations From The Trailer

  • Vice City — the main metropolitan hub. Densely urban, art deco, neon-drenched.
  • Vice Beach — the beach strip and boardwalk area (a South Beach analog).
  • Port Gellhorn — a rural coastal town shown via a fireworks scene.
  • Leonida Keys — an island chain south of the mainland, clearly modeled on the Florida Keys.
  • The Everglades — wetlands with airboats, gators, and rural trailer parks.
  • Ambrosia — a rural interior town suggested by license plate details.

Rumored Locations From Leaks

The 2022 Rockstar hack included pre-alpha development footage showing internal map fragments. From that footage and subsequent corroboration, expect:

  • A second smaller city, potentially inspired by Tampa or Orlando
  • NASA-inspired space center on the east coast
  • Multiple functional airports (unlike GTA V's single main airport)
  • A massive interior wilderness, larger than GTA V's Blaine County

Interiors and Interactivity

One of the trailer's most exciting hints: seamless interior transitions. In GTA V, most buildings were exterior-only, with interiors gated to specific mission scripts. GTA 6 appears to let players enter far more buildings organically. Expect functional gas stations, diners, motels, strip clubs, gyms, and residential homes.

Underwater and Aerial

Given Florida's coastal geography, expect substantial underwater exploration — coral reefs, shipwrecks, and cave systems. Aerially, expect helicopters, planes, and possibly commercial airliner interactions on longer flights.

💡 Key Takeaway

GTA 6's map is expected to be 2–3× the size of GTA V, spanning the entire fictional state of Leonida with Vice City as its centerpiece, the Everglades and Leonida Keys as major regions, and multiple secondary towns. This is the largest open world Rockstar has ever built.

GTA 6 Characters & Story — What We Know

For the first time in over 25 years, Grand Theft Auto is centering a female protagonist. That single decision — plus the dual-protagonist structure — tells us more about the story than any leak has.

Lucia — The First Female Protagonist

Lucia is playable, front-and-center, and the first mainline female GTA protagonist ever. The trailer opens on her being released from prison, hinting at a backstory that involves incarceration, possibly wrongful, possibly deserved. Her ethnicity appears Latina, her demeanor is guarded but sharp. Voice actor speculation currently points at Manni L. Perez (unconfirmed).

This is a legitimately significant creative choice. Rockstar has been criticized for years about the treatment of women in its games, and Lucia is the studio's most direct answer. Early behavior we can infer from the trailer suggests she is not a supporting character being retrofitted into playability — she is a written, driven, morally complicated lead.

Jason — The Male Co-Protagonist

Jason is Lucia's partner. Blonde, mid-30s, ex-military look, southern demeanor. He and Lucia are shown together across multiple scenes, sometimes affectionate, sometimes tense. Their relationship is at the emotional core of the story.

Bonnie and Clyde — The Inspiration

Rockstar has publicly compared the two to Bonnie and Clyde, and that framing is instructive. Bonnie and Clyde were lovers, criminals, and mythologized outlaws whose story ended tragically. That framing suggests:

  • A romantic relationship at the story's core (not just a partnership)
  • An escalating crime spree that becomes increasingly dangerous
  • Themes of fame, media coverage, and the American mythology of the outlaw
  • A story that will probably not end happily for at least one of them

Supporting Characters From Trailer Analysis

Multiple background figures appeared in the trailer with what looked like intentional framing — meaning they matter:

  • A middle-aged Latino man in a suit, possibly a criminal handler or lawyer
  • Multiple heavily-tattooed figures suggesting rival crime factions
  • Law enforcement in both local (Vice City PD) and federal (implied FBI/DEA) capacities
  • Influencer-type characters in beach and nightlife scenes, hinting at social-media satire

Story Setting: Modern-Day Vice City

Crucially, this is not a return to the 1980s Vice City of the original game. GTA 6 is set in modern-day Vice City, with all the contemporary satire that entails: influencer culture, gig-economy delivery apps, cryptocurrency scams, MAGA-adjacent politics, algorithmic media, and the endless performance of American life on vertical video.

Expected Themes

  • The Bonnie & Clyde myth — outlaw romance and mediated crime
  • Social media as satire — TikTok/Instagram parody woven into the world
  • The Florida absurdism — a state that generates its own news cycle
  • The American dream, rotting — Rockstar's usual heartbeat
  • Post-pandemic America — a bit older, angrier, weirder

How Dual Protagonists Might Work

GTA V introduced three-protagonist switching mid-mission. GTA 6 appears to be dropping to two, likely with tighter, more emotionally-focused switching. Expect:

  • Some missions where you play Lucia solo, others as Jason
  • Co-op missions where both are on-screen and you switch between them
  • Story-branching decisions where their perspectives conflict
  • Potentially, the entire final act as one or the other, depending on choices
💡 Key Takeaway

Lucia and Jason — GTA's first Bonnie-and-Clyde pair, its first female protagonist, its most emotionally-centered story in years. Expect the narrative to lean hard into contemporary Florida satire, social media, and the mythology of the American outlaw.

GTA 6 Gameplay — Confirmed & Expected Features

We have not seen extended gameplay yet — Trailer 1 was cinematic, not gameplay. But between engine leaks, developer LinkedIn descriptions, and Take-Two investor comments, a clear picture of the gameplay ambition has emerged.

Confirmed Features From The Trailer

  • Vehicle variety unmatched by any prior GTA — sports cars, boats, jet skis, motorcycles, airboats, semis, planes
  • Seamless interior/exterior transitions — likely no more loading screens for shops
  • Dynamic weather, including storms and flooding — Florida hurricane weather appears intentional
  • Massive NPC crowds — beach and street scenes with dozens of unique characters
  • Animal AI — gators, dogs, birds — with reactive behavior

Expected Improvements Over GTA V

Twelve years is a long time in game development. Expect fundamental overhauls to:

  • Driving physics. GTA V's driving was already brilliant. GTA 6 is expected to add proper weight transfer, tire deformation, damage that affects performance, and mud/water traction.
  • Shooting mechanics. Red Dead Redemption 2's Dead Eye and gun weight are the baseline. Expect that fidelity applied to modern weapons.
  • AI and NPC behavior. Red Dead 2 NPCs remembered you. Expect GTA 6 NPCs to be even more dynamic, with reactive memory, changing daily routines, and social-media-driven behavior.
  • Physics engine (RAGE 9). Rockstar's proprietary engine gets a full generational upgrade — fluid simulation, cloth, hair, ragdoll, and destruction all leap forward.
  • Melee combat. A perennially weak point in GTA. Expect Red Dead 2-tier grappling and blocking.

Heavily-Rumored New Features

  • Property buying and management. Not just static safehouses — active businesses you run, upgrade, and defend.
  • In-game social media. A TikTok-like app, an Instagram-like app, an X/Twitter-like feed. NPCs will post about your crimes in real time.
  • Dynamic weather and seasons. Hurricane events, seasonal beach crowds, storm flooding you can drive through.
  • Improved wanted system. Reworked so that police are more strategic, less "spawn behind you"; witnesses matter, disguises matter, hiding spots matter.
  • Interior looting. Robberies where you actually enter, threaten NPCs, and grab specific items.
  • Improved character customization. Weight, muscle, tattoos, hair growth over time (a Red Dead 2 carryover).

Online Mode: GTA Online 2

GTA Online generated over $8 billion in revenue for Take-Two. There is zero chance GTA 6 launches without a successor. Expected features:

  • Modernized progression system, learning from what worked and didn't in GTA Online
  • Deeper business management systems
  • Better matchmaking, better anti-cheat, better session privacy
  • Cross-platform play between PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
  • Potentially a delayed launch — Rockstar may release online 1-3 months after singleplayer

SSD Requirement — Why It Matters

The PS5 is architecturally built around its NVMe SSD, and GTA 6's world-streaming is going to assume that speed. A traditional HDD will not meet the game's texture streaming demands — expect the game to either refuse to install on HDD or run with severe pop-in. An NVMe SSD is going to be mandatory for a good experience, and probably enforced.

Storage: 150 GB And Rising

Red Dead Redemption 2 shipped at 105 GB and grew to over 150 GB with updates. GTA V's PC install with Online updates now exceeds 100 GB. Expect GTA 6 to launch around 150 GB and grow to 200 GB+ within a year as online content lands.

💡 Key Takeaway

An NVMe SSD is going to be non-negotiable. Plan for 150 GB of fast storage, 32 GB of RAM, and a modern mid-tier GPU (RTX 3070 tier or better) for a comfortable 1440p60 experience.

Frequently Asked Questions About GTA 6

What is the GTA 6 release date?
Rockstar Games has officially confirmed November 19, 2026 as the release date for GTA 6 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
Will GTA 6 be on PC?
Rockstar has not confirmed a PC version at launch. History strongly suggests a PC release will follow six to eighteen months after console launch, as happened with GTA V (19 months) and Red Dead Redemption 2 (13 months). Expect a PC announcement in mid-to-late 2027.
Will GTA 6 be on PS4 or Xbox One?
No. GTA 6 is a current-generation only title, releasing on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The technical ambitions — density of NPCs, physics fidelity, and streaming — exceed what last-gen hardware can deliver. There will not be a last-gen port.
How much will GTA 6 cost?
The Standard Edition is expected to cost $69.99 USD in line with current AAA pricing, with a Deluxe Edition around $89.99 – $99.99 and a Collector's Edition between $149.99 and $199.99. Rockstar has not confirmed pricing yet, but these figures track industry norms.
Can you pre-order GTA 6 now?
Pre-orders are not open yet. Based on Rockstar's pattern with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, pre-orders typically open three to six months before launch. Expect them to go live between April and July 2026, likely alongside Trailer 2 and the specific date reveal.
Where is GTA 6 set?
GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida — a satirical version of modern-day Florida — with Vice City returning as the central metropolitan area. Trailer 1 confirmed Vice City itself, Vice Beach, the Everglades, Port Gellhorn, and the Leonida Keys (a Florida Keys analog).
Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?
For the first time in the mainline series, GTA 6 features a female protagonist named Lucia, alongside a male co-protagonist named Jason. Rockstar has described their relationship as inspired by Bonnie and Clyde — lovers and outlaws on an escalating crime spree.
Will there be a GTA 6 Trailer 2?
Yes. Rockstar has confirmed additional trailers will follow ahead of launch. Historically, Trailer 2 arrives roughly three to six months before release, so a spring or summer 2026 drop is highly likely. Trailer 2 traditionally reveals the exact release date.
How big is the GTA 6 map?
Credible leaks and analyst estimates place the GTA 6 map at approximately two to three times the size of GTA V's Los Santos and Blaine County combined — roughly 150–200 km² — spanning Vice City, its suburbs, the Keys-inspired islands, and the Everglades wetlands.
Is GTA 6 online multiplayer confirmed?
Rockstar has not officially announced a GTA Online successor, but it is considered a certainty given the enormous financial success of GTA Online (over $8 billion in revenue). Expect it to launch shortly after the singleplayer release, or possibly a few months later — Rockstar did the same with GTA Online in 2013.
